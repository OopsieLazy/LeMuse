# Code of conduct

LeMuse is a small, open-source project for people who draw and design. Be the kind of person you'd want next to
you in a life-drawing class.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone was one.
- Critique work and code, not people. Say what you'd change and why.
- Credit artists and sources. Never post someone's work as your own.
- Respect privacy: don't share anyone's personal details, tokens or private channels.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, insults, sexualised comments about people, hate speech, threats, doxxing, and posting other people's
private information or work without permission.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through GitHub's private reporting form on
<https://github.com/OopsieLazy/LeMuse/security> (Security → Report a vulnerability) and start the title with
"Conduct:". Only the maintainer can read it. Reports are handled in confidence.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people from the project,
for as long as is needed to keep it a welcoming place. This applies in the repository and in any project space.
