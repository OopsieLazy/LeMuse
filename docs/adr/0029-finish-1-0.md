# ADR-0029: Finishing 1.0: session polish, museum label, browse

## Context

The owner chose the path-to-1.0 items 3, 4 and 5 (PLAN): "build 3, 4, 5". Their sources:
- the design/art review: remember where you were, view comfort, a museum-style label, sharper museum images;
- the browse items that were planned since v0.9 (grid, search, Saved, rename and remove topics).

## Decision

**3. Session polish and view comfort**
- **Remember where you were.** `ui.json` keeps the view (as a `sourceKey`), the page, the image and the study
  aids.
  - At launch the view and page come back.
  - The image comes back once that view has loaded; an earlier view still on screen can't consume it.
  - A topic or playlist that's gone is skipped.
  - A stored block id must be a positive whole number. It's never clamped to another image.
- **Pose clock:** a **large ring** (default), small, or hidden (Settings → Practice).
  - The ring is an SVG circle with r = 100/2π, so the dash is a percentage. (The W3C checker doesn't allow
    `pathLength` on circles.)
- **A flash on each new pose:** an accent frame that fades in 700 ms, for quiet rooms. It's off with
  reduced motion, or in Settings.
- **No blank flash between images:** the image fades in once loaded. A CSS animation shows it after 1.2 s
  anyway, so a missed load event can't hide it.
- **Surround** (Settings → Image): the theme's own grey, or five zero-chroma steps (white, light, mid, dark,
  black). It's a `data-surround` attribute overriding `--stage`.
- **Undo instead of warnings:** Hide this image, Mark as nudity and removing a ♥ show **Undo** for 6 seconds.
- **First run:** "What are you drawing today?" offers the 12 fullest topics, Surprise me, or Just look around.
  - Shown once, for a new library only: settings that already exist mean someone who knows the app.

**4. Museum label and sharpness**
- **The label:**
  - Schema 9 adds `date_text`, `medium`, `dimensions` and `credit_line` to `blocks`. They're remote state, and
    the archive policy never blanks a known value.
  - The Cleveland connector maps `creation_date`, `technique`, `measurements` and `creditline`: one clean line,
    at most 300 characters each.
  - The migration forgets when Cleveland records were last seen, so the next refresh reads them once more.
    Live: 1,986 of 1,992 records gained a label, with 0 errors.
- **What the caption shows:**
  - creator;
  - a **CC0** badge (open-access museum works) or **© creator** (rights belong to the creator, for personal
    reference);
  - source;
  - "date · medium · dimensions";
  - the credit line in italics.
  - Compact windows keep the slimmer bar: the label lines are hidden there.
  - Alt text adds the date.
- **Sharpness:** on a window larger than about 1,200 × 1,000 device pixels, Cleveland slides show the
  3,400 px "print" image, with the 900 px "web" image as the first fallback.
  - It's the same host (already in the CSP), with at most 2 images preloaded and nothing written to disk.

**5. Browse**
- **Grid view (W):** the current view's page (up to 500) as a contact sheet inside the image frame.
  - It uses small sizes (Are.na's `small`, else `medium`, else the display image) with `loading="lazy"` and
    `content-visibility: auto`.
  - Pick one to play from it; Esc goes back.
- **Search (/ or the box at the top of the topic list):** `Scope::Search` over the existing FTS index (titles,
  descriptions, contents, creators).
  - Words are made safe by `fts_query`; the best matches come first.
  - Content defaults and category filters still apply.
- **Saved:** `Scope::Saved`, every image with an original in the store, newest save first. It's a list entry
  under All.
- **Rename and remove topics** (+ → the topic, "This topic"):
  - Rename checks the name and that it's free.
  - Remove asks once more and says what stays: playlists and saved files.
  - In the CLI: `lemuse rename-topic` and `lemuse remove-topic --with-channels`.

## Consequences

- Each launch writes one small `ui.json` save per image change (debounced 300 ms, atomic). That's acceptable for
  a file of a few hundred bytes.
- **Tests:**
  - Rust: the label round trip and archive rule; search and Saved with filters, counts and pages; rename and
    remove; Cleveland label mapping.
  - UI: source-key round trip and refusal, the label line, sharp images, settings sanitizing, welcome topics.
  - Layout: 100 states.
- **Deferred:**
  - search across channel names (they're not in the FTS index);
  - dragging to reorder playlists;
  - a separate grid page size.
